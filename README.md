### Join the EliteMind Android beta

**EliteMind** is an AI resume builder with an ATS score checker, 50 templates and AI writing help. The Android app is in closed testing on Google Play, and I'm looking for testers.

**How to join (takes 2 minutes):**

1. **Join the tester group:** [groups.google.com/g/elitemindconsultant](https://groups.google.com/g/elitemindconsultant)
2. **Become a tester** on your Android phone, using the same Google account: [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.airesume.app](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.airesume.app)
3. **Install** the app from Google Play

Please stay in the beta for at least 14 days. Google needs that before the app can launch publicly. Thank you!

Prefer the web? Try it at **[elitemindconsultant.com](https://elitemindconsultant.com)**
